# Biomechanics, Posture Alignment, and Musculoskeletal Wellness

## 1. Ergonomic Posture Assessment & Common Deviations
Prolonged sedentary workstation usage and screen interaction produce predictable kinetic chain compensations:

### Forward Head Posture (Cervical Hyperlordosis)
* Defined by anterior translation of the cervical spine relative to the vertical plumb line intersecting the acromion process.
* Every 1 inch of forward head shift roughly doubles the effective gravitational torque experienced by the deep cervical extensor muscles (suboccipitals, upper trapezius, levator scapulae).
* Remediation: Suboccipital release, chin tucks (cervical retractions), and strengthening deep cervical flexors (longus colli, longus capitis).

### Rounded Shoulders & Thoracic Kyphosis (Upper Crossed Syndrome)
* Characterized by internal glenohumeral rotation, protracted scapulae, and excessive thoracic curvature.
* Muscle Imbalance: Hypertonic/shortened pectoralis major/minor and upper trapezius; inhibited/weakened rhomboids, middle/lower trapezius, and serratus anterior.
* Remediation: Thoracic extensions over foam roller, scapular retractions, face pulls, and doorway pectoral stretches.

### Anterior Pelvic Tilt (Lower Crossed Syndrome)
* Characterized by forward tilt of the pelvis, increased lumbar lordosis, and protruding abdomen.
* Caused by shortened psoas and rectus femoris (from seated posture) combined with dormant gluteal complexes and lengthened lower abdominals.
* Remediation: Glute bridges, hip flexor stretches, posterior pelvic tilt drills, and dead bug core stabilization.

## 2. MediaPipe Pose Landmark Tracking Principles
MediaPipe Pose provides 33 3D skeletal landmarks. Key anatomical angles computed for posture evaluation include:
* **Craniovertebral Angle:** Angle between ear tragus, C7 vertebrae proxy, and horizontal plane.
* **Shoulder Level Imbalance:** Angle formed by left and right acromion process landmarks relative to true horizontal.
* **Torso/Spine Incline:** Line connecting mid-shoulder and mid-hip landmarks relative to the vertical gravity vector.
* **Pelvic Level:** Asymmetry between left and right anterior superior iliac spine (ASIS) landmarks.
