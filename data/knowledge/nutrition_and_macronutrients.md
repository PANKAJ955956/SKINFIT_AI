# Evidence-Based Nutrition, Macronutrient Balance, and The Gut-Skin Axis

## 1. Energy Balance & Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)
Energy balance dictates tissue accrual and oxidation:
* **Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (Standard Clinical Gold Standard):**
  * Men: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age in years) + 5
  * Women: BMR = (10 × weight in kg) + (6.25 × height in cm) - (5 × age in years) - 161
* **Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE):** Computed by multiplying BMR by the Physical Activity Level (PAL) coefficient:
  * Sedentary (desk job, minimal exercise): 1.2
  * Lightly Active (light exercise 1-3 days/week): 1.375
  * Moderately Active (moderate exercise 3-5 days/week): 1.55
  * Very Active (hard training 6-7 days/week): 1.725
* **Safe Caloric Adjustment Guidelines:**
  * For weight management: Moderate caloric deficit of 300 to 500 kcal/day (targeting safe fat loss of 0.25 to 0.5 kg per week).
  * Extreme caloric restriction (<1200 kcal/day for women, <1500 kcal/day for men) is clinically contraindicated without physician supervision due to metabolic adaptation, muscle wasting, and micronutrient deficiencies.

## 2. Macronutrient Distribution
* **Protein:** 1.4 to 2.2 g/kg total body mass for active individuals. Provides essential amino acids (glycine, proline, hydroxyproline) necessary for dermal extracellular matrix maintenance, muscle preservation, and satiety signaling (cholecystokinin release).
* **Fats:** 20% to 35% of total caloric intake. Prioritize monounsaturated fatty acids (olive oil, avocados) and long-chain polyunsaturated omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA from marine sources or ALA from flax/chia).
* **Carbohydrates & Glycemic Index:** Diets high in refined carbohydrates with a high glycemic index (GI) cause acute spikes in circulating insulin and Insulin-like Growth Factor 1 (IGF-1). Elevated IGF-1 promotes sebocyte proliferation, hyperandrogenism, and follicular hyperkeratosis, exacerbating acne breakouts.

## 3. Hydration & Cellular Osmolality
* Daily recommended baseline water intake: Approximately 30 to 35 mL/kg of body weight, adjusted for climate and physical exertion.
* Electrolyte homeostasis (adequate sodium, potassium, and magnesium ratios) ensures intracellular fluid balance and prevents hypovolemic fatigue.
